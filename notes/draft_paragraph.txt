In this work we fine-tune four pretrained encoder on the SST-3 dataset and compare they performance under different learning rate. The result show that BERT-base achieve the best validation accuracy of 0.930, while RoBERTa-base perform worse than we expected with average accuracy 0.746. We also find that all models is fail to predict the neutral class, which make the macro-F1 drop to 0.534 even the accuracy looks reasonable. This suggest that accuracy alone are not enough for evaluate a three-class sentiment task.
